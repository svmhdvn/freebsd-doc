FreeBSD is continually evolving its laptop support as part of the https://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/proj-laptop/[Laptop Support and Usability Project]. Here you will find a list of laptops that have been tested using the FreeBSD Foundation's Laptop Integration Testing tooling, which have their parts ranked from 0/2 based on how well they function and what configuration they need to work.
