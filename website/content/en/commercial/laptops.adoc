FreeBSD is continually evolving its laptop support through the
https://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/proj-laptop/[Laptop Support and Usability Project].
As part of this effort, the FreeBSD Foundation started a testing phase
to ensure the comprehensive validation of laptop functionality, known as the https://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/freebsd-laptop-testing[FreeBSD Laptop Integration Testing Project].
This page contains a matrix of compatibility information of FreeBSD on these laptops and desktops.

To contribute test results for your laptop or desktop to this matrix, please see the https://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/freebsd-laptop-testing/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md[FreeBSD Laptop Testing Contributing Guide].
